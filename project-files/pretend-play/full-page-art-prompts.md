# Full-page art prompts for each kit (Gemini)

One large picture per kit, printed as a full Letter page: a wall poster or play backdrop. Each prompt below is complete on its own; paste it as is.

**Settings:** portrait, 3:4 aspect ratio, the highest resolution Gemini offers. Save as PNG, named by the kit id in the heading (for example `dino.png`), and I'll place it in the kit.

**Why the prompts say what they say:**
- The style line matches the art already in the kits, so the new page fits in.
- A plain cream band is left across the top. I print the child's name and the shop name there, because image generators misspell text.
- No faces on food or objects, no logos, no real characters or brands.
