# Vet kit X-ray scans: Gemini Pro prompts (updated 2026-11-12)

Two sets of full-page X-ray scans, one page per animal. Each prompt below is complete: copy it whole into Gemini (Pro image model), **landscape 4:3**, the biggest size it offers. No icons, emojis or picture bubbles, just the X-ray film.

- **Set 1, little kids:** the X-ray only, no words at all.
- **Set 2, big kids:** the same X-ray with the real bone names in short labels, a title and one "Did you know?" fact.

Check the bones against the note under each prompt before keeping a picture, and redo any that get it wrong. Save them as `xray-little-<animal>.png` and `xray-big-<animal>.png`.

---

## Set 1: little kids (no words)

**1. Dog**

```
A realistic veterinary X-ray (radiograph) printed as a full-page poster for children ages 3 to 8. Glowing white and pale blue bones on a deep navy-black film, with the soft grey see-through outline of the animal's body and fur around the skeleton, like a real X-ray on a light box. The skeleton must be anatomically correct for this species: the right number and shape of bones, joints in the right places, nothing invented, nothing missing. The whole animal fits on the film with a wide margin. A rounded teal frame around the film and a clean white page border. No icons, no emojis, no picture bubbles, no stickers, no cartoon characters and no decorations: only the X-ray film. No cast shadows. The animal is a medium-sized dog standing, side view: a long skull with a jaw full of teeth, 7 neck vertebrae, 13 pairs of curved ribs, a straight spine, shoulder blade, upper and lower front leg bones, small paw bones, a pelvis, thigh bone, kneecap, lower back leg bones bending backward at the ankle (hock), and a long tail of small bones. No text, no letters, no numbers anywhere on the page.
```
