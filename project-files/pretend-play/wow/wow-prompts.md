# Wow piece prompts (Gemini app)

One realistic prop per kit, each sized for a spot on a play kitchen. In the Gemini app pick the image model, set the aspect ratio shown, paste the prompt, and save the result named by the kit id (for example `vet.png`). Drop the files in the thread (or in `pretend-play/wow/raw/`) and I cut them out and add them to the kits.

Photorealistic studio product photo, flat straight-on view, soft even lighting, no cast shadow, isolated on a pure white background with generous white margin around it.
