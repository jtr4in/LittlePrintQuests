## 1. What fits

**The measurements we could find.** Neither brand publishes sizes for each part, so the Duktig and KidKraft numbers below come from retail listings and decal sellers. Before we print "fits" claims in listing copy, it's worth checking one real kitchen with a tape measure.

**By kit**
- **Fit well, no changes needed:** Burger, Coffee, Grocery, Petshop, Post, Library, Police, Dentist, Auto. They have no appliance pieces. Their signs, menu and labels all fit on the backsplash and shelves of all three kitchens.
- **Needed a tweak (done, PR #98 merged):** Pizza and Italian. The oven page is now a 7.6 in square oven that shows through or tapes onto a play kitchen oven window, plus a separate woodpile strip for shoebox builds. The setup tips now mention play kitchens.
- **Theme kits** (dino, space, vet, royal and the rest) aren't kitchen kits. Their signs and gift-shop menu still fit a play kitchen backsplash if a kid turns the kitchen into a shop. There's nothing to fix there.
- **Idea, not built yet:** Burger and Coffee would feel more like play kitchen kits with one appliance piece each: a flat-top grill for Burger and an espresso machine front for Coffee. It needs new art. Say the word if you want it.

**Trademark note.** Writing "fits the IKEA Duktig play kitchen" or "sized for KidKraft play kitchens" in a listing is generally fine as a factual compatibility claim. Don't use their logos, don't put their names in titles in a way that suggests a partnership, and never write "official" or "IKEA approved." The photos below don't need any brand name to appear in the image. Keep any logo or brand badge out of frame, and say "shown in a play kitchen" in the listing.

## 2. Gemini prompts (paste into the Gemini app)

**How to use them.** Open a new chat for each scene. Attach the reference images listed for it, from `pretend-play/kitchen-lifestyle/refs/`, in the numbered order shown, then paste the prompt. Gemini redraws kit art loosely, so if a sign comes out garbled, reply in the same chat with *"Keep everything the same, but make the sign exactly match Image 1, letter for letter."*

The style for all of them is the round 4 look you liked: an "Instagram mom" styled shot with hands only, real cut-out paper pieces, and natural light.

### Shared style block (it's already in each prompt below)
> Photorealistic lifestyle photo for a toy shop listing, styled like a polished Instagram mom account: bright soft window light, warm neutral playroom, light oak floor, a few tasteful props, shallow depth of field, shot on a full-frame camera at 35mm. No faces and no full people, only a child's hands and forearms. The printed kit pieces are real paper or cardstock, cut neatly along their edges, slightly matte with tiny paper texture and soft shadows, taped in place with small pieces of clear tape. Reproduce each printed piece exactly as in its reference image: same colors, art and words, never redrawn, cropped, mirrored or with extra text. No brand names or logos anywhere. No real money.

### Tips that saved retries last time
- Say each piece's shape ("3:2 landscape card", "20 x 5.6 inch strip", "8 inch square"). Otherwise Gemini stretches the banner into a page or the signs into strips.
- If Gemini paints the reference numbers ("Image 2") into the scene, reply: *"Remove any written labels like 'Image 2'; keep everything else identical."*
- If the kitchen comes out with a visible brand badge or logo, reply: *"Remove any logo or brand badge from the kitchen."*
- If you want the exact art (no Gemini redraw), send me the scene you like and I'll swap the real pieces in with the round 4 restore tools.
