# Shop kit card photo prompts

Model: gemini-3-pro-image, aspect 4:3, 2K. Attach the reference images in the order listed (they are in refs/, cut from the Maya sample kit), then paste the prompt. Crop the result to 1354x994 for the site card.

To rerun all of them: `bash runall.sh` from this folder (uses lifestyle/tools/round4/gen_ref.py).

## Pizza Shop

Attach: Image 1 = refs/pizza-pizza.png, Image 2 = refs/pizza-sign.png, Image 3 = refs/pizza-oven.png

```
Create a photorealistic lifestyle photo for a product card, landscape 4:3: a perfectly styled pretend pizzeria that a creative Instagram mom set up for her child in a bright, airy Scandinavian home, soft natural window light, warm neutral palette, light oak and white surfaces, styled like a premium Etsy listing photo. Close, slightly overhead three-quarter view. The star of the photo is a big round paper pizza on a wooden peel being topped with felt toppings, large and centered, filling about half the frame so it reads clearly even as a small thumbnail.

The printed paper pieces in the images above were printed on cardstock, cut out neatly along their outlines with scissors and used as real parts of the play. Reproduce each one exactly as given (same artwork, colors and words), cut to its own shape, flat paper with a subtle paper edge. Leave out any instruction text, 'best on cardstock' badges and page titles; show only the cut-out artwork:
- Image 1 (round pizza, already cut along the dashed slice lines): lying on a real wooden pizza peel on the counter, the slices pulled slightly apart, with felt pepperoni, felt basil and felt cheese being placed on top.
- Image 2 (shop sign, a 3:2 card NOT a long strip): standing in a small wooden easel frame behind the pizza.
- Image 3 (brick oven): glued onto the front of a cardboard box in the soft-focus background, with a real arched opening cut where the oven mouth is.

Props: small wooden bowls of felt toppings, a linen tea towel, a rolling pin, a small plant. A small child's hands in a striped sleeve add a felt pepperoni.
No cash register, no real money. No faces (a child's hands and sleeves only). No other text or logos anywhere. Every printed piece must be sharp, uncovered and facing the camera as much as possible. Uncluttered, airy composition with soft shadows.
```
