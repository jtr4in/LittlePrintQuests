Pizza Shop pretend play for kids

Everything your child needs to run a pretend pizzeria. Print on regular Letter paper, cut along the dashed lines, and line up some stuffed animals to be hungry customers. Here’s how to set it up, games to play and a printable kit with your child’s name on every page.

Make a personalized Pizza Shop kit

How to set it up

  1. Tape the pizzeria sign and Open sign by the “front door”.
  2. Set up the register on the counter.
  3. Lay out the toppings in small bowls or cups with their labels.
  4. Use a baking tray or a shoebox as the pizza oven.
  5. Pin on the badges and take the first order!

Games to play

  • 🍕 Make it match

    A customer picks an order card. The chef builds that pizza with the cut-out toppings.

  • 🛵 Delivery!

    Fill in a delivery ticket and drive the pizza to a customer in another room.

  • 🪙 Pay at the register

    Add up the menu prices and pay with pizza coins. Great for early counting.

  • ⭐ Pizza Club

    Stamp a club card after every pizza. A full card earns a free slice!

Who’s who

Hand out the jobs so everyone gets a turn, grown-ups and stuffed animals included:

  • Pizza Chef
  • Cashier
  • Server
  • Delivery Driver
  • Dough Maker
  • Topping Expert
  • Taste Tester

For toddlers and preschoolers

Little ones love pretend play before they can read. Give them the job with the most doing, like pizza chef, keep the money to a few big coins and let them circle pictures on the order forms. Five to ten minutes of play is a win at this age.

Questions

What age is pizza shop pretend play for?

Ages 2 to 8. The little kids version (ages 2 to 4) has big pictures, thick cut lines and order forms they fill in by circling, so no reading or writing is needed. Ages 5 and up get the writing version.

Do I need anything special to print it?

Regular Letter paper works for everything. Cardstock makes the money, menus and props last longer, and sheet protectors turn the activity pages into wipe-clean pages.

How do I set up a pretend pizza shop at home?

Tape the pizzeria sign and Open sign by the “front door”. Set up the register on the counter. Lay out the toppings in small bowls or cups with their labels. Use a baking tray or a shoebox as the pizza oven. Pin on the badges and take the first order!
