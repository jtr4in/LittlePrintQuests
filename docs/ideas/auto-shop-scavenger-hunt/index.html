Auto Shop scavenger hunt for kids

A rhyming auto shop treasure hunt for ages 3–7. Read the story, hide the clues around the house and let the junior mechanics follow the trail to the wrench. Here are free clues to start with, and a printable version with your child’s name and your own hiding spots.

Make a personalized Auto Shop hunt

Start with the story

Read this out loud before the first clue. It turns “go find some cards” into a mission.

Garage Emergency

Calling all Junior Mechanics!

This is Mechanic Max at the Auto Repair Shop. Today is our busiest day ever, with a long line of cars waiting for tune-ups and car washes! But this morning Tilly the Tow Truck backed up a little too fast and bumped our big red toolbox. CRASH! The wrenches, the screwdrivers and even the Golden Wrench rolled away.

Tilly feels terrible, so she helped me tuck eight Tool Tag cards along the way. Each one has a rhyming riddle that tells you where to search next.

Good mechanics are careful, helpful and great at working as a team. Walk, don’t run, take turns, and honk “Beep, beep!” every time you find a card. Find every tool, and a shiny surprise is waiting at the end!

Your first Tool Tag card is attached. Grab your toolboxes, mechanics!

Keep on rolling,
Mechanic Max, Head Mechanic

5 free rhyming clues

Write each riddle on a card and hide it at the answer to the clue before it. The first clue goes in the kids’ hands after the story.

  1. Clue 1

    Where are the pencils, the paper, and pens?
    Check by the desk, and then count to ten!

    Answer: Desk. Hide it: Under the keyboard or a notebook.

  2. Clue 2

    Where does the car sleep when it’s done for the day?
    Check in the garage, but don’t drive away!

    Answer: Garage. Hide it: Just inside the garage door.

  3. Clue 3

    Where do you sleep when the moon’s shining bright?
    Look under the pillow where you snuggle at night!

    Answer: Bed. Hide it: Under the pillow.

  4. Clue 4

    Sneakers and boots standing all in a row,
    Check where the shoes rest when they’re done on the go!

    Answer: Shoe Rack. Hide it: Inside a shoe on the shoe rack.

  5. Clue 5

    Where do the cartoons and movies all play?
    Check by the TV, but don’t watch today!

    Answer: TV. Hide it: Under the TV remote.

Between clues, read a auto shop rhyme to keep the story going:

Honk, honk! Mechanics, the cars need a fix,
But the tools rolled away from a tow truck’s tricks!

A wrench! You found it! Now give it a twist,
There are more tools to find on the work order list!

One tool is left, the Golden Wrench, wow!
Find it and the cars can all be fixed now!

Make it right for their age

  • Ages 3 to 5: add a picture of the hiding spot to every card so pre-readers can solve it, and keep the trail to 6 spots.
  • Ages 6 and up: let the kids read the riddles themselves, use 8 to 10 spots and add a silly challenge at each stop.
  • A big group: give each child a turn to read a clue or find a card so everyone gets a win.

Questions

What age is a auto shop scavenger hunt for?

This hunt is written for ages 3–7. Younger kids follow the picture on each card while a grown-up reads the rhyme; older kids can read the clues on their own.

How many clues should a scavenger hunt have?

Six to ten clues is the sweet spot for ages 3 to 8. It takes about 15 to 25 minutes, long enough to feel like a quest and short enough that nobody loses interest.

Can I do this hunt in a small apartment?

Yes. Pick spots in every room and zig-zag between them. The apartment trail below uses eight spots every apartment has.
