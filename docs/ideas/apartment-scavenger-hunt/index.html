Apartment Scavenger Hunt: 8 rhyming clues for kids

No stairs, no yard, no problem. Eight hiding spots that every apartment has, so the hunt fits in one floor and still feels like a big adventure.

Make this trail with my child’s name

The trail, clue by clue

Hide each card at the answer to the clue before it. The first clue goes in the kids’ hands, and the prize waits at the last answer.

  1. Clue 1

    You open it wide when a friend comes to play,
    It says “hello” and “goodbye” every day!

    Answer: Front Door. Hide it: Taped to the inside of the front door.

  2. Clue 2

    Sneakers and boots standing all in a row,
    Check where the shoes rest when they’re done on the go!

    Answer: Shoe Rack. Hide it: Inside a shoe on the shoe rack.

  3. Clue 3

    Where do we snuggle for movies and naps?
    Check under the cushions, it’s there, perhaps!

    Answer: Couch. Hide it: Under a couch cushion.

  4. Clue 4

    Where do the stories sit snug in a row?
    Peek by the books, where the readers all go!

    Answer: Bookshelf. Hide it: Tucked between two books.

  5. Clue 5

    Where do we gather for breakfast and treats?
    Look by the table with all of the seats!

    Answer: Kitchen Table. Hide it: On (or under) the kitchen table.

  6. Clue 6

    It’s big and it’s cold and it hums all day long,
    It keeps the milk chilly, so you can’t go wrong!

    Answer: Fridge. Hide it: Taped to the fridge door (or on a shelf inside).

  7. Clue 7

    Where do you splash with your bubbles and boats?
    Look in the place where your rubber duck floats!

    Answer: Bathtub. Hide it: Inside the (dry!) bathtub.

  8. Clue 8

    Where do you sleep when the moon’s shining bright?
    Look under the pillow where you snuggle at night!

    Answer: Bed. Hide it: Under the pillow.

Tips for a apartment hunt

  • Zig-zag between rooms instead of going in a line. Kids walk farther and the trail feels longer.
  • Keep it quiet for the neighbors: swap the “stomp” challenges for tiptoe ones.
  • Use the bathtub (dry!) and the bed for the last spots. They are big, easy to search and safe.
  • If space is really tight, run the trail twice with two teams and different prizes.

Questions

How long does a apartment scavenger hunt take?

With eight clues, about 15 to 25 minutes for kids ages 3 to 8, plus a few minutes beforehand to hide the cards.

Where should the prize go?

At the last spot on the trail. On this trail that’s the bed.
