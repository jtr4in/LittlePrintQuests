Auto Repair Shop pretend play for kids

Everything your child needs to run a pretend car repair shop and car wash. Print on regular Letter paper, cut along the dashed lines, and line up some toy cars and trucks as customers. Here’s how to set it up, games to play and a printable kit with your child’s name on every page.

Make a personalized Auto Repair Shop kit

How to set it up

  1. Tape the shop sign and Open sign by the “front door”.
  2. Mark a parking spot with the Drive In Here sign and a car wash corner with the Car Wash sign.
  3. Set up the register by the Please Pay Here sign, with chairs under the Waiting Room sign.
  4. Fill boxes with play tools and parts and stand a Shelf Label by each one.
  5. Pin on the badges and fix the first car!

Build it from a box

Make a Car Wash from a cardboard box and the pieces in your kit. Grown-ups do the cutting, kids paint, glue and play.

Games to play

  • 📋 Check-up

    Drive a car in and check its tires, lights and brakes on a Car Check-Up form.

  • 🔧 Fix it

    Fill in a Work Order, grab tools from the Toolbox and fix what’s broken.

  • 🧽 Car wash

    Follow How to Wash a Car with a sponge and a towel. Add a Super shiny sticker!

  • 🪙 Pay and go

    Add up the Price List and pay with garage coins. Hand back the keys!

Who’s who

Hand out the jobs so everyone gets a turn, grown-ups and stuffed animals included:

  • Mechanic
  • Tire Changer
  • Car Washer
  • Cashier
  • Oil Changer
  • Tow Truck Driver
  • Parts Runner

For toddlers and preschoolers

Little ones love pretend play before they can read. Give them the job with the most doing, like mechanic, keep the money to a few big coins and let them circle pictures on the order forms. Five to ten minutes of play is a win at this age.

The printable Auto Repair Shop kit has your child’s name on the signs, badges and certificate, plus play money, order forms, props and a grown-up guide full of ideas. Pick the little kids version (ages 2 to 4) or the big kids version (5 to 8), and add the activity pack of wipe-and-play pages if you like. It arrives by email in minutes.

Questions

What age is auto repair shop pretend play for?

Ages 2 to 8. The little kids version (ages 2 to 4) has big pictures, thick cut lines and order forms they fill in by circling, so no reading or writing is needed. Ages 5 and up get the writing version.

Do I need anything special to print it?

Regular Letter paper works for everything. Cardstock makes the money, menus and props last longer, and sheet protectors turn the activity pages into wipe-clean pages.
