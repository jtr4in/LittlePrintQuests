Bunny Egg Factory pretend play for kids

Everything your child needs to run a pretend Easter egg factory. Print on regular Letter paper, cut along the dashed lines, and gather some stuffed animals to be customers. Here’s how to set it up, games to play and a printable kit with your child’s name on every page.

Make a personalized Bunny Egg Factory kit

How to set it up

  1. Tape the Egg Factory sign and Factory Open sign by the door.
  2. Set up a Paint Studio with crayons and the painted egg cards.
  3. Make the bunny ears and tape the headband to fit.
  4. Line a basket or box with paper “grass” for the orders.
  5. Pin on the badges and open the factory!

Build it from a box

Make an Egg Drying Rack from a cardboard box and the pieces in your kit. Grown-ups do the cutting, kids paint, glue and play.

Games to play

  • 🎨 Egg painting

    Read a customer’s order form, then color plain eggs to match it.

  • 🧺 Basket delivery

    Pack a basket, fill in a delivery slip, and hop it along the map.

  • 🐣 Rush order!

    A chick needs yellow eggs fast! Paint, pack and hop it over.

  • 🥕 Egg shop

    Customers pay with carrot coins. Great for early counting.

Who’s who

Hand out the jobs so everyone gets a turn, grown-ups and stuffed animals included:

  • Egg Painter
  • Basket Packer
  • Delivery Bunny
  • Ribbon Tier
  • Color Mixer
  • Egg Counter
  • Carrot Chef

For toddlers and preschoolers

Little ones love pretend play before they can read. Give them the job with the most doing, like egg painter, keep the money to a few big coins and let them circle pictures on the order forms. Five to ten minutes of play is a win at this age.

Want it printed and personalized?

The printable Bunny Egg Factory kit has your child’s name on the signs, badges and certificate, plus play money, order forms, props and a grown-up guide full of ideas. Pick the little kids version (ages 3 to 5) or the big kids version (6 to 8), and add the activity pack of wipe-and-play pages if you like. It arrives by email in minutes.

Questions

What age is bunny egg factory pretend play for?

Ages 3 to 8. The little kids version (ages 3 to 5) has big pictures, thick cut lines and order forms they fill in by circling, so no reading or writing is needed. Ages 5 and up get the writing version.

Do I need anything special to print it?

Regular Letter paper works for everything. Cardstock makes the money, menus and props last longer, and sheet protectors turn the activity pages into wipe-clean pages.

How do I set up a pretend bunny egg factory at home?

Tape the Egg Factory sign and Factory Open sign by the door. Set up a Paint Studio with crayons and the painted egg cards. Make the bunny ears and tape the headband to fit. Line a basket or box with paper “grass” for the orders. Pin on the badges and open the factory!
