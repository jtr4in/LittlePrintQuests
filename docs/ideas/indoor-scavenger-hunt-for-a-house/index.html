Indoor Scavenger Hunt for a House: 8 rhyming clues for kids

A trail that sends kids up and down the whole house, from the front door to the laundry and back to bed, with the prize hiding at the end.

Make this trail with my child’s name

The trail, clue by clue

Hide each card at the answer to the clue before it. The first clue goes in the kids’ hands, and the prize waits at the last answer.

  1. Clue 1

    You open it wide when a friend comes to play,
    It says “hello” and “goodbye” every day!

    Answer: Front Door. Hide it: Taped to the inside of the front door.

  2. Clue 2

    Where do the jackets hang out on a hook?
    Open the closet and take a quick look!

    Answer: Coat Closet. Hide it: In a coat pocket inside the closet.

  3. Clue 3

    Where do we snuggle for movies and naps?
    Check under the cushions, it’s there, perhaps!

    Answer: Couch. Hide it: Under a couch cushion.

  4. Clue 4

    Up and down, step by step, one, two, three,
    Check by the stairs, now what will you see?

    Answer: Stairs. Hide it: On the bottom step.

  5. Clue 5

    Where are the crackers and cereal kept?
    Peek in the pantry where snacks have all slept!

    Answer: Pantry. Hide it: Next to the cereal in the pantry.

  6. Clue 6

    Head to the spot where wet clothes become dry,
    It spins and it tumbles, so give it a try!

    Answer: Dryer. Hide it: Inside the dryer drum (door open).

  7. Clue 7

    Where do you splash with your bubbles and boats?
    Look in the place where your rubber duck floats!

    Answer: Bathtub. Hide it: Inside the (dry!) bathtub.

  8. Clue 8

    Where do you sleep when the moon’s shining bright?
    Look under the pillow where you snuggle at night!

    Answer: Bed. Hide it: Under the pillow.

Tips for a house hunt

  • Spread the clues across floors. A trip up the stairs and back is half the fun.
  • Walk the route once before the party and tuck each card where a small child can reach it.
  • Close doors to rooms that are off limits so nobody wanders off the trail.
  • Put a grown-up near the stairs if your hunters are 3 or 4.

Questions

How long does a house scavenger hunt take?

With eight clues, about 15 to 25 minutes for kids ages 3 to 8, plus a few minutes beforehand to hide the cards.

Where should the prize go?

At the last spot on the trail. On this trail that’s the bed.
