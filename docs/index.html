Why it matters

The best part is playing together

Pretend play and treasure hunts are how kids practice the big stuff, and the afternoons they remember are the ones you joined. No brainstorming, no Pinterest rabbit hole: step into their world and be the hero of the living room.

🧠 Play is how they learn

Running a shop or cracking a clue means planning, taking turns, finding words and handling a little frustration. Pediatricians call play essential, not extra.

💛 You’re the best part

The back-and-forth between you and your kid builds their brain and your bond. Your attention matters more than any toy.

📱 You don’t have to plan it

Questions

Before you print

Is anything shipped to me?

No. You get a PDF to print at home on regular Letter paper, in color or black and white.

How fast will I get it?

Usually within a few minutes of checkout. It shows on screen and arrives by email, so it works even the morning of the party.

Is there a version for younger kids?

Yes. Pick Little kids (3 to 5) or Big kids (6 to 8) when you order. The little kids version has thicker cut lines, bigger pictures, picture clues in the hunt and order forms they fill in by circling pictures. The big kids version has more to read and write, plus a word search in the activity pack. Same price either way.

Can my 3-year-old do it?

Yes. For ages 3 to 5, every clue card also shows a picture of the next spot, so kids who can’t read yet can solve it themselves while a grown-up reads the rhyme.

What if my house doesn’t have a spot on the list?

Type any spot you like, such as “the hammock” or “Dad’s guitar”, and we’ll write a rhyme for it. We skip anything unsafe for little kids, like the oven.

How many kids can play?

One trail works well for 2 to 10 kids. For bigger groups, read each clue aloud and let a different kid find each card.

I’m not great at pretend play. Will this work for me?

Yes, that’s what the Field Kit is for. It gives you lines to say in character, questions to ask, customers for their shop and hints when they’re stuck, so you can follow along instead of making it up. Kids mostly want you there, not a performance.

What is the Field Kit? Do I need an app?

The Field Kit is a private page for your phone that comes with every order, with a tab for each thing you get. For a hunt it shows where each card goes, what to say in character, quick challenges and hints if the kids get stuck. For a play kit it has pretend-play scenarios, lines to say and games, and for a party pack a planning checklist. It opens in your phone’s browser from the link in your email, so there’s nothing to install.

How does the Family Pass work?

One payment of $39.99 gets you 6 orders to use within 12 months. Each order can be the whole bundle: the scavenger hunt, its play kit, the activity pack and the party pack, in any theme and for any of your kids. Your code arrives by email. Make an order as usual and type the code under “Have a Family Pass code?” on the checkout step. It covers anything in the order builder, shop play kits included; handprint art is sold separately. It doesn’t renew by itself.

What if something looks wrong?

Reply to your order email and we’ll fix it and send a new PDF free. If we can’t make it right, we’ll refund you in full. See our happy party promise.

How do I pay?

Apple Pay, Google Pay or any major card, through Stripe’s secure checkout. We never see your card number.
