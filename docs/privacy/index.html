Privacy

We only ask for what we need to make your printables, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Your order details: your child’s first name, age, theme, hiding spots, pets or toys you list, and party details for the invitations. We use them only to make your PDFs and Field Kit page.
  • Your email and name from checkout, to send your download link and answer you.
  • Free sample sign-ups: your email, to send the sample and the occasional party idea. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Who helps us

  • Stripe handles payments. We never see or store your card number.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Anthropic (Claude) helps write rhymes for hiding spots we haven’t seen before. It gets the order details above, never your payment or email.
  • Railway hosts the server that builds and stores your PDFs.

We don’t use advertising trackers, and we don’t sell or share your information with anyone else.

Kids’ information

This site is for parents and other grown-ups. We only know what you tell us about your child, which is usually a first name and age, and it’s used only to print on their pages.

Photos you take in the Field Kit stay on your phone. They’re never uploaded to us or anyone else, and the photo PDF is made on your phone too.

How long we keep it

We keep your order so your download link and your Field Kit keep working, and so we can fix it if you ask. To have your order or email deleted, write to hello@littleprintquests.com and we’ll remove it.
