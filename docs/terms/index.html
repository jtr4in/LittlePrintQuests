The Family Pass

One payment covers six orders made within 12 months of buying. Unused orders expire after 12 months, and the pass never renews or charges you again. See refunds.

Safety

Hunts are meant to be run by a grown-up. We skip unsafe hiding spots like the oven, but you know your home best, so please check each spot before you hide a card. Little cut-out pieces aren’t for children who still put things in their mouths.
