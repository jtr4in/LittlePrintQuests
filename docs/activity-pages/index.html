New: fold it yourself

An origami page in every theme

Each pack opens with a big square of paper printed in the theme’s pattern, and a page that shows how to fold it into something: a unicorn, a rocket, a volcano, a pirate ship.

  • Three to five big folds, easy enough for a 4-year-old with a grown-up nearby.
  • Cut-out pieces to glue on at the end, like the unicorn’s horn and mane.
  • “Circle the shape” questions along the way: triangle, square or rectangle?
